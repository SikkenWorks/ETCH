ETCH FULL SYNTH / V1.37

ETCH V1.37 adds a dedicated USB-MIDI keyboard when SEQ is held during power-on: all 27 keys send note-on/off on MIDI channel 1, OCT buttons transpose, and the synth audio/analog output remain disabled. All V1.36 ARP7 fixes remain. V1.37 also preserves the full caption fits, every turn produces a distinct stored phrase, and a running ARP restarts immediately without requiring a screen change. Prior POLY safeguards remain: EVOLUTION, ORGANIC, SPECTRAL, LIVING and PATCH BLEND are hidden and bypassed in POLY, with all saved values restored in MONO. A staged 8/6/4/3/2 voice budget reduces the chance of permanent AUDIO STOP on overload. The FM1 chip has not been retested; software-only validation. The firmware stores ORGANIC, LIVING, MOD MATRIX, EVOLUTION, SPECTRAL and PATCH BLEND controls independently with each named preset in PJT27. The PRESETS encoder sets BPM (30–240) outside the named browser, with a temporary BPM overlay. GLO-held PC6 startup diagnosis from V1.29 remains available. PATCH BLEND and RIFF 2.0 remain supported. PATCH BLEND mixes three drawn waveforms and core sound parameters between two already-saved presets without overwriting either source. Open PATCH BLEND by cycling LFO past MOD MATRIX: K1 PATCH A, K2 PATCH B, K3 BLEND (0–100), K4 ACTIVE. RIFF LAB follows CLOCK in the ARP page cycle: K1 DENSITY, K2 MUTATE, K3 VARIANT, K4 NEW RIFF. RIFF stays synced to the shared project BPM, K2 rate division and configured MIDI clock. PJT27 stores advanced settings per preset, retains RIFF variations and reads PJT25 and earlier projects. V1.27 USB-stability and analog-output startup safeguards, the protected boot header and OTA loader are retained. Experimental, software-tested only: physical installation, independent recovery and startup-pop elimination remain unverified. Back up data and do not flash without independently proven restoration.

ETCH FM-1 R2 TEST TARGET - V0.24
================================

THIS DIRECTORY IS NOT A FLASH APPROVAL.

V0.24 contains real AC791N/pi32v2 target source plus an audited ELF -> app.bin
and offline .fwsc.lab package pipeline. It intentionally does NOT bundle a
hardware-verified fm1_sdk_bridge.cpp, CRT/startup, linker script, or a normal
release .fwsc.

ETCH's safety specification requires ROM-level recovery, two byte-identical full
flash dumps and two successful complete stock restores on the exact target unit
before original ETCH firmware is flashed.

Passing the app/package validators proves structural consistency only. It does
not prove the image will boot safely on the physical FM-1.
